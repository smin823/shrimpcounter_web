Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

ShrimpCounter is designed to work without an account. We collect only the limited information needed to run the app, show ads in the free version, manage subscriptions and fix problems. This Privacy Policy explains what is collected, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who We Are

ShrimpCounter is provided by Plolabs ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy applies to the ShrimpCounter mobile app. If you have any questions, contact us at contact@plolabs.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Do Not Need to Give Us

ShrimpCounter has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app.

2.2 Subscription Information

If you buy a Premium subscription, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. To check whether your subscription is active we use RevenueCat, which receives a random app user ID, the product you bought and the start, renewal and expiry dates of the subscription. We never receive your card number or other payment details.

2.3 Usage and Device Information

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used. It collects information that does not directly identify you, such as an app instance ID, which screens and features are used, the device model, operating system version, language and approximate region.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the app, including the free daily quota and Premium subscriptions.
  • To understand how features are used and to improve the app.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To show advertisements in the free version.

4. Photos

Photos you take or import for shrimp counting are analyzed entirely on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers and we cannot see them. Photos and counts you keep are stored in the app's album on your device only, and you can delete them there at any time. A photo leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it yourself.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

5.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

The free version shows advertisements through Google Mobile Ads. Google may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; advertising ID on Android)
  • Device information (device type, operating system, screen size)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

On iOS the app asks for your permission before any tracking for personalized ads. You can change this choice, or reset or limit your advertising ID, in your device settings. Premium subscribers do not see advertisements.

5.2 Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability. When the app crashes or hits an error, it may collect the device model, operating system version, the state of the app at the time, stack traces and the time of the crash. This is used only to find and fix bugs.

5.3 Other Providers

These providers process the information described above under their own privacy policies: Google (Firebase and AdMob), RevenueCat, Apple and Google Play. To prepare the on-device model, the iOS app may contact Apple once after installation; no photos are sent.

6. Keeping and Deleting Data

  • Photos, counts and notes are kept on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Because there is no account, uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • To ask about or request deletion of analytics or subscription records linked to your app user ID, contact us at the address below.
  • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

7. Children

ShrimpCounter is a tool for professional and general use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You can make a request at any time by contacting us at contact@plolabs.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the law changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact Us

Email: contact@plolabs.com
