Prepare Your Shrimp

Spread the shrimp in a shallow tray or basin with a plain background for best results.

2

Take a Photo

Open ShrimpCounter and take a photo, or pick one from your gallery. The AI marks and counts every one.

3

Get Your Count

See the count in seconds. Results are saved to your album for record-keeping.
